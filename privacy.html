Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Where your data lives

ClipParty runs on your own computer. Videos, clips, settings and your connection to TikTok are stored there. The developer does not run a server for ClipParty and does not receive your videos, clips or tokens.

2. TikTok data

When you connect TikTok, ClipParty receives an access token, a refresh token and your basic profile information (display name) through TikTok Login. The tokens are stored locally on your computer and are used only to show which account is connected and to upload or post the clips you send. When you upload or post, ClipParty sends the video file, the caption and the privacy setting to TikTok. ClipParty requests the permissions user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.

3. Optional services you connect yourself

Depending on your settings, audio or text of your videos may be sent to a transcription or AI provider (for example Groq, OpenAI or Anthropic) to create transcripts, clip suggestions and captions, using your own API key. If you connect a Dropbox folder as a source, ClipParty reads the videos in that folder. These services process data under their own privacy policies. If you do not set them up, nothing is sent to them.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not use analytics or tracking in ClipParty.

5. Your control

You can disconnect TikTok in the app and remove ClipParty's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Deleting the app's local folder removes all locally stored data, including tokens, videos and clips.

6. Children

ClipParty is not directed to children under 13 and requires a TikTok account, which TikTok only offers above its minimum age.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.

ClipParty

A personal desktop tool for turning long videos into short clips.

ClipParty runs on your own computer. It takes long videos you have the rights to use, finds the best moments, cuts them into short vertical clips and adds captions. When you choose to, it publishes those clips to your own TikTok account, either as a draft or as a direct post.

How it works

  1. You add a source video (a local file or a folder you connect).
  2. ClipParty transcribes the video and proposes clips.
  3. You review the clips and their captions.
  4. You connect your TikTok account with TikTok Login and approve the permissions yourself.
  5. ClipParty uploads the clips to your account using TikTok's Content Posting API.

TikTok permissions we ask for

  • user.info.basic: to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload: to send clips to the drafts of your account.
  • video.publish: to post clips to your account, with the caption and privacy setting you choose.

You can disconnect at any time. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
